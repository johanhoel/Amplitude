Home Deck app

The home panels as an app, at home and away. Lights, the front door, who's home and the buses.

Android

Download the app
  1. Open the downloaded file. Android asks to allow installing from your browser: allow it, then Install.
  2. Open Home Deck. Ask the home admin for a pairing code, then tap Scan QR code (or type the 6 digits).
  3. Optional: add the Home Deck widget (long-press the home screen → Widgets), and the tiles All lights off, Lock front door and Wake PC (swipe down twice → ✎ edit).

Updates come by themselves: the app offers them when you open it.

iPhone

  1. Ask the home admin for a pairing code and scan the QR code with the Camera app (on the home Wi-Fi).
  2. In Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen.
